About Trip Tracker

Your personal travel companion — map, explore, and relive every journey.

🌍 What is Trip Tracker?

Trip Tracker is a travel journaling platform that helps you document and visualize every trip you take. Whether it's a weekend road trip or a month-long adventure abroad, Trip Tracker makes it easy to keep a beautiful, map-based record of your travels.

🗺️ Interactive Maps

See all of your travels on a single interactive map. Zoom into countries, states, and cities you've visited. Explore your personal travel history with rich, visual detail.

📸 Photos & Memories

Attach photos to your trips and revisit your favourite moments. Each destination comes to life with the images you capture along the way.

🔍 Explore & Discover

Browse attractions, landmarks, and hidden gems in every destination. Trip Tracker surfaces Wikipedia content and curated highlights so you can plan your next adventure.
